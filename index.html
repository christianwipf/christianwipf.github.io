Christian Wipf

I am an Economist at the Financial Stability Division of the Austrian National Bank. I obtained my PhD at the University of Bern in 2020.

My research interests are in macroeconomics, monetary economics, financial stability, banking and financial economics.

I have recently worked on narrow banking and CBDC proposals, the current inflationary spike and optimal inflation targets.

Please see my cv and my resume for more details.
